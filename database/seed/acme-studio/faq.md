# Frequently asked questions (DEMO)

## How long does a website take?

A Starter website usually takes three to four weeks from the kickoff call to launch.

## Do you work with clients outside your city?

Yes. Acme Studio works with clients remotely and holds meetings by video call.

## Who owns the website after launch?

The client owns the website, its content and its domain name after the final payment.

## What are your office hours?

The office is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00.
